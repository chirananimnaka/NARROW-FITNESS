What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It's a useful indicator of whether you're at a healthy weight.

BMI Categories:

Underweight: < 18.5
Normal: 18.5 - 24.9
Overweight: 25 - 29.9
Obese: ≥ 30
